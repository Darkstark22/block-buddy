Move one square at a time

Use arrow keys or W A S D on a keyboard. On a phone or tablet, tap the on-screen arrow pad below the board.

•ᴗ•→→★

Push, don’t pull

Stand behind the block and move toward it to push. If it reaches a wall or a corner, you may need to start over.

✦→▥·↔

Use the course mechanics

Touch a ✦ switch to open its gate. Red ↔ walls shift each turn; they pause if you or the block are in their next spot.
